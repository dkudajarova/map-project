five separately delineated footprint matches are consistent with Harvard's
building history. Barnard Hall's Hail record describes it as a rear building,

not forced onto the adjacent Briggs footprint.

The Cabot House Wikipedia article was incorrectly assigned to Holmes Hall
(`206-8`), which belongs to Pforzheimer House. It is now assigned to Cabot Hall
(`206-12`), the named building within the Cabot complex.
